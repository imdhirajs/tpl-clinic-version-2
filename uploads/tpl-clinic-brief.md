## 1. Business Overview

**Name:** TPL Clinic (also referred to as "The PRP Lab")
**Website:** [tplclinic.com](https://www.tplclinic.com)
**Location:** 22 Seymour Street, Mayfair, London, W1H 7HY
**Contact:** info@tplclinic.com · 020 7998 7077 · Instagram [@tplcliniclondon](https://www.instagram.com/tplcliniclondon) · WhatsApp booking line

**What they are:** A medical aesthetics clinic positioned as the UK's first and leading clinic specialising in **PRP (Platelet Rich Plasma)** treatments. Non-surgical focus (aside from a hair transplant offering). Awarded "Specialist Aesthetic Clinic of the Year 2026/27."

**Core philosophy:** They don't rely on single stand-alone treatments — they combine multiple treatments (e.g. PRP + dermal fillers + wrinkle relaxation injections + LED phototherapy + radiofrequency) because this combination approach is scientifically proven to outperform any one treatment alone.

**Who it's for:** People dealing with skin ageing, hair thinning/loss, or body skin concerns (cellulite, scarring, stretch marks) who want non-surgical or minimally invasive results.

## 4. Current Website & Business Model (as found on site)

- No online treatment booking — deliberate choice (see meeting notes below).
- Booking flow available on the site is for **consultations only**, with a fee taken upfront; refundable if cancelled 48+ hours ahead.
- Contact form on homepage triggers an email to the clinic + an on-screen confirmation popup to the user ("we will contact you").
- Testimonials displayed directly on homepage (patient quotes about PRP facials, lips, Sofwave — one reviewer says they cancelled a facelift after trying Sofwave there).
- GDPR and Terms & Conditions pages exist — clinic is compliance-conscious (relevant given healthcare-adjacent services).
- Services page uses a hand-drawn icon for each treatment, linking to individual treatment pages — client likes this and doesn't necessarily want it changed.

## 5. Meeting Notes — Pitch/Discovery Call (10 Aug 2026)

### Current booking process (manual, and staying manual)
- Clinic does **not** take bookings online today — intentional, because they want control over the diary.
- Process: client calls → staff quotes available times → client pays a deposit online → staff books them in manually.
- After-hours calls become missed calls; the clinic calls back the next day.
- Dhiraj raised the idea of an **AI voice agent** to handle after-hours calls and bookings automatically.
- **Client declined firmly** — said it's "not genuine" and does not want an AI agent taking calls. Wants to keep this manual and human. This should not be re-pitched to this client.

### Design direction — hero concept
- Client initially asked for a "3D interactive" page (something the user can rotate/manipulate, using cloud/physics-based rendering).
- Dhiraj clarified the distinction: true 3D (interactive, rotatable models) vs. the "cinematic luxury" style shown on his Instagram, which is actually a **video-based scroll animation** (not real-time 3D).
- Client confirmed they actually want the **cinematic scroll-animation style** (like Dhiraj's portfolio examples), not true 3D.
- **Concept agreed:** A human body/anatomy visual as the hero. As the user scrolls, the camera/video moves through different body regions, each tied to a treatment category:
  1. **Face** → face treatments
  2. **Hair** → hair treatments
  3. **Stomach** → fat-loss / body treatments
  4. **Arm** → diagnostics/blood tests offered by the clinic
- Framed as primarily a **camera movement/scroll-video direction** effect rather than true interactivity.
- Dhiraj proposed creating a **storyboard** before building, so the client can visualize the concept ahead of full production — standard step in his process for new projects.

### Scope discussed
- Client's real long-term want: a **full website redesign**, all pages.
- Agreed to **start with just the landing/hero page** first, to see how it goes, given time and cost considerations of doing the whole site.
- **Services page:** client already likes the current icon-based layout. Doesn't need to change unless it visually clashes with the new hero style — open to revisiting later if there's time/budget.
- **No backend actions/functionality needed** beyond what already exists — confirmed static one-pager style with the current WhatsApp click-to-chat button retained.
- **Contact form:** keep current behavior as-is (email notification to clinic + on-screen confirmation popup for the user) — client already happy with this, no changes requested.
- **Testimonials:** client wants these **removed** from the redesigned page — "the whole page needs to be a new design."
- **Copy/content:** client is fine with Dhiraj writing all new copy from scratch; client will review and edit afterward as needed.

### Visual direction / theme
- **Light theme** confirmed (not dark) — "for sure," to stay consistent with the rest of the existing site.
- Palette: **gold, white, gray** tones — but gold should be used sparingly/subtly, not "in your face." Mostly white-and-gray-led with gold as an accent.

### Pricing discussion
- Dhiraj's standard quote for a cinematic scroll-animation **landing page only** (hero section + landing page, not full site): **£600 GBP**.
- Client pushed back, saying their budget expectation was **£400–450**.
- Negotiated down: Dhiraj's final offer/floor = **£500 GBP maximum** — cannot go lower than this.
- No payment has been made yet; client is waiting to see the demo before deciding.

### Timeline discussion
- Standard turnaround quoted: **~10 days maximum**.
- Can be compressed if urgent: **5–7 days**, or even **as fast as 1 day** in a rush scenario (with the caveat that revisions add time — Dhiraj noted that fast personal demos with no revisions aren't the same as a paid client project where feedback/changes are expected).
- Client asked about the very fast (30–60 minute) builds mentioned on Dhiraj's Instagram — Dhiraj clarified those don't involve client revision cycles, which is why real projects take longer.

### Tools/stack mentioned
- Dhiraj mentioned using **Cloud (Claude Code)** and **Vercel** as the primary stack, with occasional use of other tools depending on the use case (client specifically asked and Dhiraj confirmed a mixed toolset, not just one single tool).

### Access & handover model
- Dhiraj will connect a **GitHub repo to Claude Code** and give the client access.
- **First month of changes/edits included for free** — Dhiraj will make any requested changes directly, ideally via a **Google Meet call** so the client can watch/learn the process.
- From month 2 onward, the client can make their own changes using prompts (self-serve), having learned the workflow during month 1.
- Client raised a concern about ongoing cost/independence — doesn't want to keep coming back to Dhiraj for every small change, and was probing whether hosting on Vercel/Claude is effectively free for Dhiraj (Dhiraj clarified that revision cycles still cost time/credits even if base hosting is cheap).

### Client's decision status
- Client is currently **also talking to another designer/developer** (unspecified, unknown origin) and wants to compare before committing.
- Client's explicit ask: if Dhiraj can send a **brief demo/storyboard showing how it will look**, they said they can decide immediately — "here's the money, let's go" — and could pay and start the same day if happy with it.

### Commitment made on the call
- Dhiraj promised to send a **storyboard + brief demo the same day** (10 Aug 2026), to try to close the deal quickly while the client is still deciding between designers.
